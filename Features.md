# 🚀 Elevator Management ERP System - Complete Features

## 📌 **Executive Summary**

The **Elevator Management ERP System** is a comprehensive, cloud-based enterprise solution designed specifically for elevator installation, maintenance, modernization, and service companies. Built with cutting-edge web technologies and Firebase infrastructure, this system provides complete business management from sales pipeline to billing, with real-time GPS tracking, offline capabilities, and multi-language support.

**Powered by Ambivare Solutions** - Your Complete Elevator Business Management Partner

## 🎯 **Key Highlights**

✅ **21 Integrated Modules** - Complete end-to-end business management
✅ **Role-Based Access Control** - 5 distinct user roles (Admin, Sales, Technician, Reception, User)
✅ **Offline-First Architecture** - Work seamlessly without internet connectivity
✅ **Real-Time GPS Tracking** - Live technician location monitoring with Mapbox integration
✅ **Multi-Language Support** - English, Hindi (हिंदी), Marathi (मराठी)
